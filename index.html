Where the answers live

Not in the noise.

Not in the speed.

In the vast, uncomfortable quiet.

Every man carries something

he has not yet found the words for.

The work

He does not need more advice.

He needs an hour.

To sit with it.

The answers are incomplete.

And still —

it is yours to solve.

Somewhere between who you were and who you are becoming —

is an hour that belongs to you.

Take it.

The answers are incomplete. And still — it is yours to solve. — W
