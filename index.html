The Period

The period after WILL is not punctuation. It is the brand. It signals intention — a declaration that is complete, certain, and not open for discussion. In all standard applications the period is rendered in gold #C5A55A — on black, charcoal, and cream backgrounds alike.

The single exception is the Special application — black letterforms on a gold background. In this case the period renders in black #0D0D0D to maintain contrast and legibility against the gold field. The identity of the period is preserved through context — the gold background itself carries the meaning.

The period is never omitted. Never scaled differently from the letterforms. Never removed for any reason.

Do Not

I WILL.

Do not render in sans-serif or system fonts. Cormorant Garamond Light only.

I WILL.

Do not use bold or heavy weights. The mark is always font-weight 300 — Light.

I WILL.

Do not render the period in cream or any color other than gold #C5A55A.

02   Color System

Black

#0D0D0D

Primary background. The foundation everything lives on.

Charcoal

#1C1C1C

Secondary background. Section differentiation.

Cream

#E8DCC8

Primary text. Headlines. The declaration voice.

Gold

#C5A55A

The standard. Accents. Labels. The period. Used sparingly.

Muted

#9A8E7E

Secondary text. Italic copy. The reflective voice.

Hierarchy

Gold speaks first — it is the label, the accent, the standard marker.

Cream speaks second — it is the declaration, the headline, the product name.

Muted speaks third — it is the description, the reflection, the supporting thought.

Black and charcoal hold everything.

Usage Rules

Gold is used for labels, rules, accents, and the brand period only. It is never used for large blocks of body text.

Cream is the primary reading color. All headlines and declarations are cream.

Muted is for body copy, descriptions, and anything italic or secondary.

Never place cream directly on gold. Never place gold directly on cream.

03   Typography

The Typeface

Cormorant Garamond is the only typeface used across the entire I Will FITFO brand. No exceptions. It is used at weights 300 (Light), 400 (Regular), and 500 (Medium) in both roman and italic variants.

Cormorant Garamond was chosen for its combination of classical authority and elegant restraint. It reads as both literary and luxury — exactly the tension this brand lives in.

04   Brand Voice

Restrained. Intentional. Powerful.

The man this brand speaks to does not need to be sold to. He needs to be recognized. Write to that man. Recognize him. Then give him the invitation.

Write This

Some journals record what you smoked. This one records who you are becoming.

Direct. Specific. Creates immediate recognition in the right reader.

Not This

This beautifully crafted premium journal is designed for the discerning cigar enthusiast who wants to document their tasting journey.

Generic. Performative. Talks at the reader instead of to him.

Write This

For the man who carries more than he says.

Sparse. Earned. The reader fills in his own story.

Not This

We believe every man deserves a space to reflect on his personal journey and document his experiences in a meaningful way.

Corporate. Empty. We believe is never how this brand speaks.
